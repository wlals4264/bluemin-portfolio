# 이번 생성 대상: company

Create ONE new 3D asset for the concept: COMPANY (회사 프로젝트 — a project built as an employee
at a company, as opposed to a personal side project or a team side project).

Use the attached Profile asset ONLY as the visual Style Master.
Do not copy its subject or its flat identity-card composition.

This asset belongs to the same "Soft Spatial 3D — v1" icon family.

CONTEXT:
This icon sits directly next to a small pill-shaped text badge that already reads
"회사 프로젝트" (company project). The badge text is the authoritative label — this icon
is a purely decorative concept accent shown at a very small size (as small as 32px), often
right next to that badge. The silhouette must read as "a company / an office" at a glance,
even that small — do not rely on any detail that only reads at large size.

SUBJECT:
A small physical office building (a simplified low-rise building block), NOT a person,
NOT a briefcase, NOT a logo mark.

FORM:
- a single rectangular building volume, softly rounded corners and edges (no sharp corners,
  matching the family's soft-plastic/glass geometry)
- slight 3/4 perspective so the front face and one side face are both visible, with visible
  depth/thickness (a real 3D volume, not a flat card)
- a simple flat or very slightly pitched roof line
- 2–3 small rounded window shapes on the front face, arranged in a simple grid (soft rounded
  squares, not real window frames or glass panes with visible interior)
- one small rounded rectangle suggesting a door/entrance at the base, centered on the front face

MATERIAL:
- the building's main volume: warm ivory soft plastic (same tone as the Style Master's base)
- the windows: translucent pale-blue glass panels (small, simple, no interior detail)
- smooth rounded geometry, polished but not metallic
- soft specular highlights, same material language as the Style Master

STYLE:
- premium soft 3D icon
- minimal
- friendly
- clean
- slightly toy-like but not childish
- same material language as the attached Style Master

CAMERA:
- orthographic-like
- 3/4 front perspective, slightly elevated view
- centered composition
- no dramatic perspective distortion
- same camera angle as the Style Master

LIGHTING:
- soft top-left key light
- subtle cool rim light
- gentle ambient illumination
- same lighting character as the Style Master

IMPORTANT DIFFERENTIATION:

This must look like a small PHYSICAL BUILDING / OFFICE OBJECT.

It must NOT look like:
- a person, a badge, or an identity card (that concept already exists as "profile")
- a laptop, phone, or browser window (those concepts already exist as "laptop" / "mobile" / "web")
- a briefcase or a suitcase
- a real logo, wordmark, or any readable text
- a skyscraper or a large complex building — keep it a small, single, simple volume

COMPOSITION:
- one building only
- centered
- generous transparent padding
- no text
- no logo
- no surrounding scenery (no trees, no road, no sky, no ground plane beyond the object's own
  built-in soft shadow)

OUTPUT:
- square 1:1 image
- transparent background
- isolated object
- no black background
- no environment
- no floor
- no external glow baked into the background
- preserve clean translucent glass edges on the windows

Priority:
1. recognizable small-office-building silhouette, readable even at 32px
2. consistency with Soft Spatial 3D v1
3. clearly distinct from "profile", "laptop", "mobile", "web"
4. minimal detail
