# 이번 생성 대상: team

Create ONE new 3D asset for the concept: TEAM (팀 프로젝트 — a project built together with other
people, as a small team, as opposed to a solo personal project or a company-assigned project).

Use the attached Profile asset ONLY as the visual Style Master.
Do not copy its subject or its flat identity-card composition.

This asset belongs to the same "Soft Spatial 3D — v1" icon family.

CONTEXT:
This icon sits directly next to a small pill-shaped text badge that already reads
"팀 프로젝트" (team project). The badge text is the authoritative label — this icon is a
purely decorative concept accent shown at a very small size (as small as 32px), often right
next to that badge. The silhouette must read as "more than one person / a small group" at a
glance, even that small.

The attached Style Master ("profile") already represents a SINGLE person as one rounded
head-and-shoulders shape on a flat identity card. This new "team" asset must be immediately
distinguishable from that single-person asset by showing MULTIPLE simplified person-tokens
together — that multiplicity is the entire point of this icon.

SUBJECT:
A small cluster of 3 simplified, abstract person-tokens (NOT a flat identity card, NOT a
single figure).

FORM:
- three small, simplified rounded person-tokens, each built the same way: a small rounded
  sphere/capsule head shape sitting on a slightly wider rounded body/shoulder shape underneath
  (the same minimal head+shoulders language as the Style Master's single figure, just repeated
  three times as separate small 3D tokens, not silhouette cutouts)
- arrange the three tokens close together in a tight, friendly cluster (e.g. two slightly
  forward and one slightly behind/between them, gently overlapping), so they read as ONE group
  composition, not three separate unrelated icons scattered in the frame
- vary the three tokens' heights very slightly (subtle front/back or left/right offset) so the
  grouping feels three-dimensional and clustered rather than a flat repeated row
- no flat card or badge backing behind them — these are freestanding rounded 3D tokens, unlike
  the Style Master's card-mounted figure

MATERIAL:
- warm ivory soft plastic for the body/head shapes of the tokens (same tone as the Style
  Master's figure)
- optionally, translucent pale-blue glass accents at the base/contact points where the tokens
  touch, to keep the family's glass material present without adding a flat card
- smooth rounded geometry, polished but not metallic
- soft specular highlights, same material language as the Style Master

STYLE:
- premium soft 3D icon
- minimal
- friendly
- clean
- slightly toy-like but not childish
- same material language as the attached Style Master

CAMERA:
- orthographic-like
- 3/4 front perspective, slightly elevated view
- centered composition
- no dramatic perspective distortion
- same camera angle as the Style Master

LIGHTING:
- soft top-left key light
- subtle cool rim light
- gentle ambient illumination
- same lighting character as the Style Master

IMPORTANT DIFFERENTIATION:

This must clearly read as MULTIPLE people together, at a glance, even at 32px.

It must NOT look like:
- a single person or a flat identity card (that exact concept already exists as "profile" —
  do not reuse its flat card-mounted single-figure composition)
- faces, facial features, hair, or any individually identifiable character
- a real logo or any readable text
- a large crowd — exactly three tokens, no more

COMPOSITION:
- exactly three person-tokens, clustered together as one group
- centered
- generous transparent padding
- no text
- no logo
- no surrounding scenery

OUTPUT:
- square 1:1 image
- transparent background
- isolated object
- no black background
- no environment
- no floor
- no external glow baked into the background
- preserve clean translucent glass edges on any glass accents

Priority:
1. immediately reads as "a small group of people", clearly distinct from "profile" (single
   person), readable even at 32px
2. consistency with Soft Spatial 3D v1
3. the three tokens feel like one clustered composition, not scattered separate icons
4. minimal detail
