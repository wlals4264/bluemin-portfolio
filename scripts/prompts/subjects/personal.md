# 이번 생성 대상: personal

Create ONE new 3D asset for the concept: PERSONAL (개인 프로젝트 — a project built entirely alone,
on one's own initiative, as opposed to a company-assigned project or a project built with a team).

Use the attached Profile asset ONLY as the visual Style Master.
Do not copy its subject or its flat identity-card composition.

This asset belongs to the same "Soft Spatial 3D — v1" icon family.

CONTEXT:
This icon sits directly next to a small pill-shaped text badge that already reads
"개인 프로젝트" (personal project). The badge text is the authoritative label — this icon is a
purely decorative concept accent shown at a very small size (as small as 32px), often right
next to that badge. The silhouette must read as "an independent, self-driven, solo endeavor" at
a glance, even that small.

The attached Style Master ("profile") already represents "a person / identity" as a
head-and-shoulders figure on a flat card — do NOT reuse a person/figure shape for this asset,
that concept is already taken. This asset must use a completely different, non-figurative
object so that "profile" (identity), "team" (a cluster of person-tokens) and "personal" (this
asset) remain three visually unmistakable silhouettes.

SUBJECT:
A small toy rocket, launching upward — a widely understood symbol for an independent,
self-driven "personal project" (an indie/side-project spirit), rendered as one physical object.

FORM:
- a simplified rocket body: a rounded, slightly tapered cylindrical body ending in a soft
  rounded nose cone at the top
- two or three small rounded fin shapes at the base, softly rounded, not sharp
- optionally, one small round window/porthole on the body, roughly in the upper third
- a soft, rounded, upward-tapering silhouette overall (nose cone up), so its orientation
  reads instantly
- slight 3/4 perspective, matching the rest of the family, with visible volume/depth
  (a real 3D object, not a flat cutout)

MATERIAL:
- the main body: warm ivory soft plastic (same tone as the Style Master's base)
- the nose cone and/or porthole window: translucent pale-blue glass
- smooth rounded geometry, polished but not metallic
- soft specular highlights, same material language as the Style Master

STYLE:
- premium soft 3D icon
- minimal
- friendly
- clean
- slightly toy-like but not childish
- same material language as the attached Style Master

CAMERA:
- orthographic-like
- 3/4 front perspective, slightly elevated view
- centered composition
- no dramatic perspective distortion
- same camera angle as the Style Master

LIGHTING:
- soft top-left key light
- subtle cool rim light
- gentle ambient illumination
- same lighting character as the Style Master

IMPORTANT DIFFERENTIATION:

This must look like a small PHYSICAL TOY ROCKET.

It must NOT look like:
- a person, figure, or identity card (that concept already exists as "profile"; a cluster of
  figures already exists as "team" — this asset must not use any person-like shape at all)
- a building (that concept already exists as "company")
- a real spacecraft, missile, or anything realistic/technical — keep it soft, rounded and toy-like
- fire, smoke, or exhaust trail effects — keep the object isolated and static, no motion FX
- a real logo, wordmark, or any readable text

COMPOSITION:
- one rocket only, nose pointing up
- centered
- generous transparent padding
- no text
- no logo
- no surrounding scenery (no stars, no clouds, no launch pad, no ground plane beyond the
  object's own built-in soft shadow)

OUTPUT:
- square 1:1 image
- transparent background
- isolated object
- no black background
- no environment
- no floor
- no external glow baked into the background
- preserve clean translucent glass edges

Priority:
1. immediately reads as "a small rocket", clearly distinct from "profile", "team", "company",
   readable even at 32px
2. consistency with Soft Spatial 3D v1
3. no person-like shape anywhere in this asset
4. minimal detail
